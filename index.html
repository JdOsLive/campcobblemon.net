Membership

How to join

You’ll need to be 18 or older and have your application approved before joining the server.

Read the camp rules
  1. 1Join the Discord

    Find server announcements, chat with players, and submit your application.

    Open Discord ↗
  2. 2Apply in #apply

    Fill in the two-page application in #apply. Staff review it, and Counselor Bidoof DMs you the decision.

  3. 3Install the modpack

    Get the latest version in the Modrinth app while you wait for approval.

    Get the modpack ↗
  4. 4Connect to the server

    Once approved, connect to play.campcobblemon.net.

Counselor Bidoof handles the backend in Discord — it takes your application, opens a thread for it, posts events and trophies, and reports whether the server is up. Staff read every application and make the call; the bot files it and sends you the answer. Art by @SirPlnguin.
