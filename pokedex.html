Pokédex

Everything here is custom to the server and doesn't spawn anywhere else. Costume Bidoofs are Bidoofs in outfits — each with its own typing, ability and stats. Campion forms are regional variants with their own types and stats. The new evolutions branch off Pokémon you already know.

Each card lists the biomes or places it spawns in and how rare it is there. Open one for the full picture, and add two to compare them before a doof tournament.

Heads up: the costume Bidoof typings, abilities and stats shown here are from datapack v2.0, which isn't on the server yet. Until it is, they're still plain Normal-type Bidoofs in game.
